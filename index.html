Recommended Immediate Farmer Action

Practical, organic & field-tested steps to save your harvest

1
Prune & Safely Dispose Leaves

Cut heavily spotted leaves immediately with clean shears. Burn or bury them outside the field. Never throw into compost.

2
Stop Overhead Watering

Water only at soil level using drip or channel irrigation. Wet leaves allow fungus spores to multiply rapidly.

3
Apply Organic Spray / Bio-fungicide

Spray 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) or Copper Oxychloride (2.5g per liter of water) early morning before heat peaks.

🌱 Organic Remedy

Trichoderma viride @ 5g/liter root drenching + 5% Neem oil spray every 7 days.

🧪 Standard Remedy

Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2g/liter or Chlorothalonil spray on dry foliage.

🌿 Prevention Tips
Field Best Practices

How to Protect Your Crop

Follow these essential agronomic practices to stop disease outbreaks before they harm your yield.

🌱 Crop Care

Certified Seeds & Crop Rotation

Always sow certified disease-free seeds. Rotate Solanaceous crops (Tomato, Potato, Chilli) with legumes or cereals every 2–3 seasons to break pathogen lifecycles in the soil.

💡 Golden Rule: Never plant tomato after potato in the same field.
💧 Water Management

Smart Drip & Morning Irrigation

Irrigate crops at soil level using drip systems. Water early in the morning so incidental leaf moisture evaporates quickly with the morning sun, preventing fungal spores from germinating.

💡 Golden Rule: Avoid overhead sprinkler watering during cloudy weather.
🍃 Leaf Management

Bottom Pruning & Good Airflow

Prune all leaves within 20 cm (8 inches) of the ground once plants reach 1 foot tall. Clean lower stems prevent rain-splash from kicking fungal spores from wet soil onto leaves.

💡 Golden Rule: Disinfect pruning shears in 70% alcohol or soap between plants.
🌾 Field Hygiene

Weed Eradication & Clean Mulch

Keep bunds and field borders free from wild nightshade weeds which act as wild disease hosts. Spread dry straw mulch around plant beds to shield lower foliage from direct soil contact.

💡 Golden Rule: Deep-plow your field in summer to expose spores to heat.
🔍 Regular Monitoring

Bi-Weekly Morning Field Walks

Inspect underside of older leaves twice a week between 7 AM and 9 AM. Early detection allows low-cost organic remedies to stop diseases before they damage flowers and fruit.

💡 Golden Rule: Pay extra attention after rainy spells or sudden humid nights.
🛡️ Bio-Protection

Preventative Bio-Agents & Neem

Treat seeds with Trichoderma viride or Pseudomonas fluorescens before sowing. Spray 5% cold-pressed Neem oil preventatively every 10–14 days during high humidity periods.

💡 Golden Rule: Bio-agents work best as preventatives before disease appears.
